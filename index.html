Process

A structured road to launch.

01
Discovery & Scope

Understanding your business metrics.

We kick off with a call detailing the outcomes the website needs to facilitate (sales, bookings, signups). We define technical boundaries, scope the exact features, and deliver a transparent price breakdown.

02
Figma Blueprinting

Visualizing the layouts.

I build structured prototypes in Figma, showing you exactly how content organizes and translates across desktop and mobile screens. You receive mockups to review before writing code.

03
Clean Engineering

Fast, responsive coding.

Translating approved Figma layouts into production-ready responsive code. I construct features (bookings, carts, checkouts) with high performance, accessibility, and neat semantics.

04
SEO & Hand-off

Setting you up for growth.

We configure domain, hosting systems, structured tags, and schema. I hand over the site with written/video guidelines, enabling you to manage copy or images without writing code.
